Ragi Sangati

Ragi Sangati is a famous and healthy dish from South India. It is made with finger millet flour (ragi) and cooked rice. People shape the mix into soft balls. It gives long-lasting energy and strength.

Erra Karam Dosa

Erra Karam Dosa is a fiery, popular South Indian breakfast from Andhra Pradesh, especially the Rayalaseema and Nellore regions. It features a crispy dosa spread with a pungent, spicy red chili-onion paste (erra karam) and often finished with a savory roasted gram podi and ghee

Jonna Rotte

Jonne Rotti (most commonly known as Jolada Rotti in Kannada or Jonna Rotte in Telugu) is a traditional, unleavened Indian flatbread made from sorghum millet flour. It is a staple food deeply rooted in the food culture of North Karnataka, as well as parts of Maharashtra, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
